# Light Industrial Attendance Policy

## Points

Points are received under this Attendance Policy for an Unexcused Absence, Tardy, Leave Early, or for not following the Call-In requirement as set forth in this Policy.

Points will accrue on a 12-month rolling period. This rolling period begins the date an employee receives each point. Individual points will drop annually, i.e., 12 months after the date they were issued.

**Example:** An employee receives a point for an Unexcused Absence on November 15, 2023; that point will drop from the employee’s attendance record 12 months later, on November 15, 2024.

## Unexcused Absence

An Unexcused Absence is any full-day absence that is not an Excused Absence.

Each Unexcused Absence will be one (1) point. Two (2) or more consecutive days of Unexcused Absences for medical reasons will be one (1) point, as long as each day of the absence is covered by a note from a healthcare provider. Any day of a continuous absence that is not covered by a note from a healthcare provider will be a separate Unexcused Absence.

**Example:** If an employee has an Unexcused Absence for medical reasons from November 1st through November 3rd and provides a note from a healthcare provider covering only the absence from November 1st through November 2nd, the employee will receive two (2) points: one (1) for the absence from November 1st through November 2nd, and one (1) for the absence on November 3rd.

## Tardy and Leaving Early

An employee is Tardy if the employee is not on an Excused Absence and clocks in after the start time of their established shift.

An employee Leaves Early if the employee is not on an Excused Absence and leaves the premises before the employee’s scheduled end time.

Points accrue for being Tardy and Leaving Early as follows:

- Tardy by four (4) hours or less = one-half (0.5) of a point.
- Tardy by more than four (4) hours = one (1) point.
- Leaving Early by four (4) hours or less = one-half (0.5) of a point.
- Leaving Early by more than four (4) hours = one (1) point.

## Overtime

Scheduled voluntary or required overtime will be treated as any regularly scheduled worktime for the purposes of this Policy. Once an employee has agreed to work voluntary overtime, or has been assigned required overtime, the employee will become subject to the provisions of this Policy for that overtime.

## Call-In Procedure for Unscheduled Absences

Employees are expected to schedule absences ahead of time whenever possible. Employees are expected to notify the Company of Unscheduled Absences or Tardies at least one (1) hour prior to the employee’s scheduled start time (“Call-In”). Failure to Call-In absences that are otherwise excused pursuant to this Policy will result in one-half (0.5) of a point.

As provided in CBA, Article XIV, Section 1:

> “In addition, employees shall be permitted to use up to five (5) days of vacation in full-day increments with at least one (1) hour’s notice to the Company. Employees will be allowed three (3) emergency Call-Ins of less than one (1) hour per year.”

Employees who Call-In Vacation pursuant to and in compliance with Article XIV, Section 1 will not receive one-half (0.5) of a point as a result.

## Job Abandonment

An employee who fails to Call-In for three consecutive days of Unexcused Absences will be deemed to have abandoned their job and voluntarily resigned.

## Holiday Pay for Holidays Not Worked

See CBA, Article XIII, Section 5 for employee eligibility to receive Holiday Pay when no work is performed.

## Inclement Weather

Employees are expected to arrive on time and work their full scheduled shift. However, in the event of severe inclement weather, an employee’s Tardiness, Unexcused Absence, and/or Leaving Early due to the inclement weather may be discussed with the Union for consideration of a potential exception.

## Discipline for Points

The discipline for points accrued in a rolling 12-month period is as follows:

| Points | Discipline |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Verbal coaching session |
| 2 | Documented coaching session |
| 3 | Documented verbal warning #1 |
| 4 | Documented verbal warning #2 |
| 5 | Written warning #1 |
| 6 | Written warning #2 |
| 7 | One (1) day suspension without pay |
| 8 | Termination |

## Perfect Attendance Incentive

If an employee does not receive any points within a 90-day period from their last point, and for each 90-day period thereafter with no points, the employee will earn one (1) point attendance credit.

The attendance credit is to be used to decrease the employee’s existing point balance by one (1) point. An attendance credit must be used immediately once earned.

An employee with zero (0) points at the end of a 90-day rolling period will earn one (1) point credit, not to exceed negative one (-1), which shall be used immediately upon their next Unexcused Absence.

## Supplemental Supported Rules — Not Part of the Original Printed Policy

> The rules below were supplied or confirmed separately by the app publisher. They are not part of the original printed attendance-policy document above.

### Reaching Seven Points Twice

**Reported rule:** If an employee reaches seven attendance points twice within one year, the employee is dismissed.

**Source status:** This rule was reported separately and is not present in the supplied printed attendance policy. “One year” is provisionally understood to mean a rolling 12-month period and still needs confirmation.

**Provisional interpretation:** Count two distinct occasions on which the employee reaches seven points within a rolling 12-month window. Until the exact written rule or an authoritative clarification is available, the app may display a clearly labeled warning but must not automatically state that dismissal is certain.

### Paid Sick Day Duration

**Confirmed operational rule:** The annual paid sick day is eight (8) hours for both first-shift and second-shift employees.

**Source status:** Confirmed separately by the app publisher. Article 36 of the supplied CBA establishes one paid sick-leave day per year in a full-day increment with no rollover, but does not specify the number of hours for each shift. The eight-hour value is therefore an operational rule and not quoted contract language.
