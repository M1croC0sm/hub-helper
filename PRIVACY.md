# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Hubb Helper is an unofficial, privacy-first Android application. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Hubbell Incorporated, Killark, the IBEW, or the IAM.

## Data handled by the app

The app may store information the user chooses to enter or import, including attendance dates and points, PTO and sick balances, call-ins, holidays, notes, work schedules, and copies of workplace documents. This information can be sensitive employment data.

## Collection, sharing, and network access

Hubb Helper does not include the Android Internet permission. It has no user accounts, advertising, analytics, crash-reporting service, or developer-hosted backend. The app does not transmit user records to the developer, an employer, or another third party. Text recognition runs on the device using the bundled ML Kit text-recognition model.

The only runtime permission requested is notification permission, used for an optional generic weekly reminder. Reminder text does not include attendance or balance details.

## Storage and protection

Records, OCR text, and imported originals are stored in app-private storage. Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for all app data. The optional app lock uses Android's biometric/device-credential prompt; the app does not receive or store biometric data.

Users may explicitly export a ZIP backup. Exported files are outside the app's private storage and are not encrypted by Hubb Helper. Users are responsible for storing, transmitting, and deleting exports securely.

## Retention and deletion

Data remains on the device until the user deletes individual records, removes documents, resets the app, clears app storage, or uninstalls the app. Resetting the app permanently removes its stored records and documents.

## Accuracy and employment decisions

OCR text and calculations are aids and may be incorrect. Original records, applicable agreements, policies, and authorized employer or union guidance remain authoritative. Hubb Helper should not be the sole basis for an employment, payroll, leave, disciplinary, or legal decision.
