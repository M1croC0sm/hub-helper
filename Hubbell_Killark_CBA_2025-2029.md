# Hubbell Incorporated Delaware, Killark Division — Collective Bargaining Agreement

**Effective:** May 1, 2025 through April 30, 2029

## ARTICLE 1 Agreement

This agreement is made by and between (1) HUBBELL, INCORPORATED DELAWARE,
KILLARK DIVISION (“The Company”); and (2) I.B.E.W., Local No. 1 and I.A.M., District No. 9
(“The Union” or “The Unions”).

## ARTICLE 2 Union Recognition

The Company agrees that it recognizes the Union as the exclusive collective bargaining
agency for all employees of the Company at its Fenton, Missouri facility, except for office clerical
employees, and for supervisory employees with authority to hire, promote, discharge, discipline or
otherwise effect changes in the status of employees, or effectively recommend such action.

The Company further agrees that all working regulations as set forth in this agreement with
respect to rates of pay, wages, hours of employment or any and all other working conditions effected
by this agreement shall apply to all employees above mentioned as constituting the bargaining unit
alike.

## ARTICLE 3 Union Security

All employees covered by the terms of this agreement shall be required to become and
remain members of the Union as a condition of employment from and after the ninety-first (91st) day
following the date of their employment (or assignment through a temporary agency), or the effective
date of this agreement, whichever is later. All employees must become and remain members of the
Union or pay their equal share of the cost of the collective bargaining and Union representation for
the life of the agreement. An employee in violation of this article shall be discharged when a written
request is submitted by the Union to the Employer.

If an employee is a successful candidate for a salaried position, that employee will retain his
or her seniority in their respective union for one (1) year.

The Employer agrees to deduct union membership dues in the amount fixed in accordance with the
bylaws of the Local Unions or the Constitution of the International Unions, provided the Employer is notified
in writing and to remit by the last of each month to the Financial Secretary of the Local Unions the aggregate
deductions made, providing that the Company has received from each member of whose account such
deductions are made, a written assignment which shall be irrevocable for a period of one (1) year from the
date thereof or until termination date of this agreement, whichever occurs sooner. Such assignment may also
provide that the authorization shall automatically be renewed and irrevocable for successive periods of one (1)
year unless revoked by written notice to the Employer and the Local Union thirty (30) days prior to the
expiration date of the one (1) year period, or each applicable bargaining agreement between the Employer
and the Union, whichever occurs sooner.

## ARTICLE 4 Non-Discrimination

     It is further agreed that there will be no discrimination in connection with any employee on account of his or her Union membership or proper Union activities.

     The Company and the Union agree that neither will discriminate against an employee because of race, creed, color, sex, national origin, age, ancestry, citizenship, military status, sexual orientation, gender identification, disability, childbirth, or any other personal characteristic protected under applicable federal, state or local law, nor because of exercise of rights guaranteed employee by Section Seven of the Labor Management Relations Act, as amended, except to the extent specifically permitted by legal provisions of this agreement.

## ARTICLE 5 Grievance Procedure

     The grievance form shall be dated and signed by the employee and must be submitted to the Company within five (5) working days of the occurrence of the event. Failure to file such a grievance within the five (5) working days shall prohibit any further consideration of the grievance.

     All grievances on the part of the employees shall be submitted in writing to the committee persons in the department where the grievance occurs. The committee persons will take up the grievance with the supervisor. If they are unable to settle it within three (3) working days, the department committee person and the supervisor (both must sign written grievance) will turn it over to the head shop steward who will in turn take it up with the personnel department.

     Should the Company fail to adjust such grievance by conference with the shop steward or shop committee within five (5) working days, such grievance shall then be referred to conference between the Company and the business representative of the Union. Should the Company and the business representative of the Union fail to arrive at a satisfactory adjustment of any grievance or dispute within five (5) working days, the matter may then be referred by either party to a Board Arbitration, provided written notice of intent to arbitrate the dispute is given to the other party within ten (10) working days. Failure to comply with the ten (10) working days by either party, the particular case in question will be considered null and a closed file.

     If the grievance is submitted to arbitration, the parties shall immediately jointly request the Federal Mediation and Conciliation Service to submit the names of seven (7) persons qualified to act as arbitrators. The Union and Employer representatives shall each have a choice of rejecting the names of three (3) persons of these seven (7) persons and the remaining seventh shall be elected as the arbitrator. The decision of the arbitrator shall be final. The expense of the arbitration shall be borne one-half by the Union and one-half by the Company.

     Employees who are shop stewards or committee persons may leave their work during their regular working hours for the purpose of adjusting grievances, provided that the shop steward gives the supervisor sufficient notice to enable the supervisor to provide another employee to relieve the shop steward in order that production may not be interrupted.

All conferences between the Company and the Union on grievances shall be held during working hours without loss of time or pay to the employees who are members of the Union.

## ARTICLE 6 Discipline and Discharge

### Section 1 — Employees may be disciplined or discharged for just cause, subject to the Grievance and Arbitration procedure.

### Section 2 — It is in the interest of the Union, the Company and the affected employee(s) that discipline be administered in a timely manner, taking into consideration all of the circumstances, such as, the nature and extent of the Company’s investigation and the availability of necessary personnel. When the Company administers the discipline, a Union representative shall be present unless the employee informs the representative that he or she chooses not to be represented.

### Section 3 — The Company further agrees that if, in the event any employee or employees may have been discharged or suspended and later found not at fault, and the charges have been incorrectly made, such employee or employees shall be paid for the time lost and reinstated to their former seniority rights and position. Investigations for violation of rules and misconduct will be made immediately without loss of time or pay to any employee or employees engaged in making such investigation; provided the Company is notified in writing five (5) working days after such discharge or layoff occurs.

## ARTICLE 7 Union Officer Privileges

The officers of the Union who request reasonable leave of absence for the necessary business demands connected with the Union, other than conferences between the Company and the Union, shall have such leaves of absence at the time requested without compensation from the Company for such time lost.

## ARTICLE 8 Seniority

It is deemed desirable to establish for employees of the Company, other than temporary employees, certain seniority rights, and to that end the following understanding and agreement has been reached.

a) Those employees who have been temporarily laid off shall report ready for work no later than the fifth working day after notice to return to work has been sent. Failure to report ready for work within such time, except in cases of proven illness or other conditions beyond the control of the employees, shall constitute loss of seniority rights.

b) Seniority shall be given effect according to a list which shall be posted. Any complaints as to seniority ratings must be made in writing to the Company within thirty (30) days after the list is posted. The Company will not be held responsible for any errors in the seniority rating of any employees if no complaint is filed within thirty (30) days of the date such employee’s name first appears on such posted list.

c) New employees shall be on probation for ninety (90) calendar days, during which time
they shall have no seniority rights and may be dismissed by the Company at any time
during the probationary period without recourse and not subject to the grievance
procedure. A probationary employee shall not receive any benefits under this Agreement
and shall not accrue seniority. Probationary employees are subject to Article 3. This
paragraph shall apply regardless of whether the employee is hired directly by the Company
or through a temporary agency.

d) Layoffs will be effected in each department or location and by departments in the inverse
order of seniority, and rehiring will be effected in the order of seniority by departments
except that where in the opinion of the Employer the employee can be used to advantage in
another department, the Company reserves the right to transfer the employee to another
department.

e) The word "department" as used in the above articles shall be construed as meaning the unit
represented by a particular Union shall be considered a department, and that seniority in
these particular departments shall be by job classification.

f) Seniority shall never be lost except through discharge, quitting voluntarily, abandoning
position, failure to answer a return to work call as specified in Paragraph (b) herein, or for
just cause agreed upon through grievance procedure, provided however, that in any case
where the employee has not worked for a year or more, the employee shall lose his or her
seniority. In case of a layoff, an employee will have recall rights for a period equal to the
employee's length of service, to a maximum of one year.

g) The Company will prepare and deliver to the Union a list of employees who are new on the seniority
list, which list shall give the names of such employees, their clock numbers and date of last hiring.
Thereafter, upon request, accredited representatives of the Union will be advised of changes in
seniority.

h) Upon application filed with the Employment office, leaves of absence will be granted
employees for a specific period of time for just cause without loss of seniority. No leaves
of absence will be granted to employees to accept employment elsewhere, and anyone
accepting such employment will be considered to have abandoned his or her job. Any
employee selected to perform duties for his or her Union, or by the International Union,
will be granted leave of absence without loss of seniority rights.

i) Upon receipt by the Company from an employee of a signed, written certificate from the treating
physician setting forth the nature and expected duration of that employee's illness, injury, or
pregnancy which the physician certifies as then incapacitating the employee from safely performing
his or her regular work duties, the Company will consider such physician's certificate as proper cause
for granting such disabled employee who requests same as a leave of absence without pay. Such
leave of absence will be granted in writing for a period of up to thirty (30) days, but which leave of
absence may, upon written request from the employee with satisfactory evidence that the disabling
medical condition continues to be of a disabling nature, be extended for an additional period or
periods of up to thirty (30) days each; but normally not to exceed a total period in excess of six (6)
months. While an employee is out on such an approved leave of absence, his or her seniority shall

continue for purposes of preserving the employee's place in line of seniority but shall not be
considered as time worked or paid time. Up to two (2) recognized holidays occurring during such
leave of absence will, however, be credited and paid for by the Company.

Upon timely submission, prior to expiration of such approved leave of absence to the
Company by the employee of a signed, written certification by the treating physician that
the employee on approved leave of absence is then able to safely resume his or her regular
work duties, as of the date stated, when the employee's physical condition has ceased to
be disabling, such employee will then be reinstated in accordance with their relative
seniority and ability for the work then available.

j) When an employee absents himself or herself from work for a period of two (2) consecutive days
without the consent of the Company, except by reason of sickness or other justifiable cause promptly
reported to the Company and recognized by them, he or she may be subject for dismissal, layoff, or
other penalty. In case of illness, the Company, upon written request, shall have the right to require
the employee to furnish a statement from the physician attending such an employee describing the
condition of the employee and stating approximately when he shall be able to return to work. Failing
to furnish such a statement within ten (10) calendar days, the employee shall be considered a
voluntary quit. Conditions beyond the control of the employee will be considered.

k) If an employee transfers temporarily into a high-paying job classification and such transfer
is full-time for a period of four (4) months, the Company shall, at the end of the four (4)
month period, post the job as permanent, except as provided for in Article 24, Section (C).
The four (4) month period may be extended by mutual agreement between the Company
and the individual Union involved when 25% or more of the total hours constituting the
temporary assignment was occasioned by absences of permanent employees, measured in
hours, within a particular job classification, as determined from the payroll.

l) Where experience and ability are relatively equal, bidding for promotions, lateral positions
and lower classified positions will be made according to seniority.

Management shall select for a forty (40) working day maximum trial period, the most
senior employee from the list of employees bidding a permanent job posting, providing said senior
employee is qualified to perform the job posted. Management shall select for trial the next most
senior employee from the list of employees bidding, when unable to fill the job with the most senior
employee.

Employees will be permitted to bid laterally and to lower classified positions on
permanent job postings, once every eighteen (18) months. If an employee successfully bids to a
lower classified position, the employee shall be paid the rate for the lower classification position.
If lateral or lower classified bidding employee is the senior qualified bidder on job posting and is
unable to perform duties during trial period, or refuses trial period, or after trial period, employee
is permitted to vacate position, employee will not be permitted to bid laterally or to a lower
classified position again for eighteen (18) months.

The employee thus selected will have the opportunity to prove his or her qualifications for the job.

Any employee who does not qualify within a maximum of forty (40) days shall be returned to their former job without loss of seniority.

Employees who sign permanent job postings, and later refuse the offer of qualifying trial, will be prohibited from signing another job posting for a period of six (6) months commencing with the date employee voluntarily states that job is not wanted.

All permanent jobs must be agreed to by the Employer, the Union and the Employee.

The Steward will be notified of the disposition of job posting within five (5) working days after removing the posting from the bulletin board.

## ARTICLE 9 Service in the United States Armed Forces or Defense Activities

With regard to military leaves, both parties agree to abide by all State and Federal Laws pertaining to this subject.

## ARTICLE 10 Mutual Responsibilities

It is conceded by both parties to this agreement that fair treatment, good service, and due diligence in the observance of the rules as promulgated by the Company will be essential to the maintenance of these working conditions and wages, understanding that such rules as may be promulgated by the Company shall not in any way conflict with the terms of this agreement. If so, it shall be subject to the Grievance Procedure.

## ARTICLE 11 Contract Booklets

The Company agrees that it will have copies of this contract printed in booklet form for distribution to all employees.

## ARTICLE 12 Leaves of Absence

It is agreed by both parties to this agreement that leaves of absence of not more than thirty (30) days duration shall be granted at the convenience of the Company without loss of seniority, provided that such leaves of absence are requested in writing and signed by the proper officials of the Company, such written leaves of absence over the signature of the proper Company official shall be made in duplicate form, one copy being retained by the Company and the other being deposited with the officials of the Union.

A leave of absence as described above, may be extended for not more than an additional thirty days (30) days, if mutually agreed upon between the Company and the Union.

Failure to return to work within three (3) working days after the expiration of a leave of absence shall be considered a voluntary quit.

## ARTICLE 13 Hours and Overtime

**Section. 1.** Eight (8) consecutive hours shall constitute a day’s work interrupted by a 30-minute lunch period, to be worked on the following schedule: 6:00 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. from Monday to Friday, with a thirty (30) minute lunch hour, inclusive; regular schedule for 2nd shift employees will be 2:15 p.m. to 12:45 a.m. with a thirty (30) minute dinner break. The Company may institute a third shift. In the warehouse, the regular first shift schedule will begin no later than 9:30 a.m. and end no later than 6:00 p.m. Changes in the regular schedule of hours may be made by agreement between the Company and the Union. First shift shall have normal starting time no earlier than 5:00 a.m.

In the event a holiday falls on a Saturday, it will be observed the Friday before; if it falls on a Sunday, it will be observed on the following Monday.

If a holiday falls on a Friday, it will allow 2nd shift employees to observe the holiday on the preceding Thursday.

Based on business requirements for the foreseeable future, the Company may change the hours of work for certain employees or an entire department for a minimum of five (5) consecutive workdays. Such hours shall begin no earlier than two hours before the standard shift or end more than one- and one-half hour after the standard shift. However, regular first shift schedules for order filling employees may begin no later than 9:30 a.m. and end no later than 6:00 p.m. The Company shall advise employees of the exact starting and quitting times at least one week in advance of the schedule change. Change in schedule will be for no less than five (5) consecutive workdays.

**Section 2.** All time worked outside of the regular scheduled hours and all time in excess of eight (8) hours in a twenty-four hour period shall be considered overtime and paid for at the rate of time and one-half for the first four (4) hours and double time thereafter.

### Section 3. With respect to weekend overtime:

a) Overtime on Saturdays shall be paid for at a rate of time and one-half.

b) The Company will post to seek volunteers to work weekend overtime three (3) days prior to the weekend. If not enough qualified volunteers fill the needed openings, the Company will notify employees of mandatory overtime two (2) days prior, by 2:00 for all shifts. For example, 1st shift will be notified of overtime for Saturday no later than 2:00pm on Thursday; 2nd shift will be notified of overtime for Friday no later than 2:00pm on Wednesday.

c) Weekend overtime must be cancelled by noon the day prior, for all shifts. For example, 1st shift will be notified of cancellation of Saturday overtime no later than noon on Friday; 2nd shift will be notified of cancellation of Friday overtime no later than noon on Thursday.

d) No employee shall be mandated to work weekend overtime more than two (2) times per
month, unless there are five (5) Saturdays in a month, during which the Company may schedule
mandatory overtime three (3) times during that month.

e) Notwithstanding sections above, the Company may schedule mandatory overtime more
than the amount prescribed above if there is unforeseen emergency or extraordinary circumstance. In
such cases, the Company will provide advance notice to the Union, and employees will be paid
double-time for hours worked during said weekends, whether mandated or not.

### Section 4 — Time worked on Sundays and the following holidays: New Year's Day, Good
Friday, Memorial Day, Fourth of July, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, the Friday immediately
following Thanksgiving Day, the last working day before Christmas, Christmas Day, a “Roving”
holiday (may be taken at employee's discretion upon five (5) days advance notice and supervisor's
approval), employee's birthday (may be taken any time during the month it occurs with supervisor's
approval), shall be at the rate of double time. No premium pay or additional compensation will
be paid to the employee who works the actual or selected birthday. In the event management requests
the employee work on birthday or day selected, payment will be made as set forth in the preceding
paragraph.

### Section 5 — Eight (8) hours straight time pay shall be paid to hourly employees, except those
employees who regularly work less than eight hours per day, for the following holidays when no
work is performed including holidays that fall on Saturday or Sunday; provided, however, that
where holidays fall on Sunday and are celebrated on a later date by the state, nation or by
proclamation, holiday pay shall be paid for the day on which said holiday is celebrated: New Year's
Day, Good Friday, Memorial Day, Fourth of July, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, the Friday
immediately following Thanksgiving Day, the last working day before Christmas, Christmas Day, a
“Roving” holiday (may be taken at employee's discretion upon five (5) days advance notice and
supervisor's approval), employee's birthday (may be taken any time during the month it occurs with
supervisor's approval), provided the following conditions are met:

1) The employee must have completed thirty (30) days of continuous service with the
Company immediately preceding the holiday

2) The employee must have worked the last scheduled workday prior to and the next
scheduled workday after such holiday, unless employee is prevented from so doing by
proven illness, a death in the employee's immediate family, or unless the employer has the
written consent of the Company covering this absence from work on the day prior to
and/or the day after such holiday. If the employee is absent on such days because of illness
or death in the employee’s immediate family, the employee must notify or cause notice to
be given to the Company at the employee’s earliest opportunity. The Company shall have
the right to examine into the facts before making the payment. Those employees who
regularly work less than eight (8) hours per day shall receive holiday pay on the basis of
the number of hours that they regularly work.

Section 5a.  When the Company determines that overtime is necessary on a three (3) day
holiday weekend, it will fill its needs on a voluntary basis, unless an employee(s) had previously
agreed to work the holiday weekend overtime.

### Section 6 — All second shift employees shall receive $1.00 as the night shift premium. All third
shift employees shall receive .40¢ per hour worked as the shift premium.

### Section 7 — Overtime work will be assigned on the basis that the employee who does the work during the
day shall be offered to work the overtime. This shall apply regardless of whether the overtime work is performed
during the week or on the weekend.  In the event that the employee who normally performs the work refuses the
overtime, the overtime work shall then be offered to the employee within the classification with the highest job
classification seniority.  In the event there is insufficient number of employees within the department where the
overtime is required who agree to work, the Company will assign the overtime to employees in the classification
in the reverse order of seniority.  The Company shall post mandatory weekend overtime by 2pm on Thursday.

It is understood that in the event the plant is shut down for a brief period, such as when a holiday falls on
a Tuesday and the plant is closed on Monday, or when a holiday falls on Thursday and the plant is closed on
Friday, or at inventory period, the Company may bring in a skeleton force to perform such work as necessary,
and such work will be assigned to the senior employee within the job classification who can perform the work
required.

### Section 8 — Any extra pay required under this contract for work on Saturdays, Sundays, or for
work outside an employee’s regular shift if occurring in work week in excess of forty (40) hours
is agreed to be not a different rate of pay, but instead a payment in satisfaction of statutory
overtime required by Federal Laws, rules and regulations.

### Section 9 — Notwithstanding the workday as described in Section 1, the parties agree that the
Company has the right to run the current ten (10) hour, four (4) day a week schedule.  For
employees working the ten-hour schedule only, the following rules apply: 1) they will be paid 1 ½
overtime for hours worked beyond 10 hours in a workday; 2) they will be paid ten (10) hours for
full vacation days five (5) hours for half vacation days; 3) they will be paid eight (8) hours for
holidays, and Company holidays will be observed when they fall; 4) shift premium will not be
included in determining the base rate for vacation days and holidays; 5) they will receive 1 ½ time
for all hours worked on the fifth day of the workweek, and double time for all hours worked on the
sixth day of the workweek; 6) all other provisions in Article 13 will apply.

## ARTICLE 14 Vacation

Procedure:  All regular full-time employees are eligible for vacation. Each year beginning on
January 1, regular full-time employees are eligible to earn vacation days. Eligibility is determined by
the employees’ years of service, according to the following:

Regular Full-Time Employees - Vacation Tiers

On January 1 of each year after hire:

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 to less than 5 years | 2 weeks (80 hours) |
| 5 to less than 10 years | 3 weeks (120 hours) |
| 10 years + | 4 weeks (160 hours) |
| 20 years + | 5 weeks (200 hours) |

New Hires

Newly-hired employees begin to earn vacation on their date of hire, to be used in their first year of employment, according to the following schedule. For example, if an employee begins work as a Hubbell employee on any date in April, he or she will receive eight (8) vacation days for the year in which they are hired.

| Hire Month | Days |
| :--- | :--- |
| January | 10 (80 hours) |
| February/March | 9 (72 hours) |
| April | 8 (64 hours) |
| May | 7 (56 hours) |
| June | 6 (48 hours) |
| July | 5 (40 hours) |
| August/September | 4 (32 hours) |
| October | 3 (24 hours) |
| November | 2 (16 hours) |
| December | 1 (8 hours) |

### Section 1 — Employees shall make written application to supervisor for vacation prior to April 15, specifying vacation dates preferred. Such application shall be acknowledged in writing prior to May 15, setting forth dates of vacation. Employees who wish to take vacation between January 1 and May 31 shall make written application to their supervisor prior to December 1, specifying vacation date preference. Such request shall be acknowledged, in writing, prior to January 1 setting forth approved date of vacation. In case of conflict of dates between employees with same job classification, the Company will, if possible, give

preference based on length of service. Employees not applying for vacation prior to April 15, will take their
vacation when convenient to the Company. Employees with vacation eligibility in excess of five (5) days
may take up to five (5) of such excess days one day at a time. Employees shall obtain approval for daily
vacation at least five (5) days in advance. Employees who have earned three (3) or more weeks vacation
may take up to ten (10) days at a time. Employees will be allowed to take five (5) days of their vacation in
half-day increments. Employees shall obtain approval with at least five (5) days notice in advance. In
addition, employees may use five (5) days or ten (10) half days, or any combination of the two, provided the
employee must let his or her supervisor know in advance, no later than before shift or no later than mid-shift.

**Section 2.** Vacations are not cumulative and must be completed each vacation period. Vacations are
earned and paid on the current year basis. The rate of pay for each week of vacation will be the base rate at
the time of vacation becoming earned and accrued. The Company recognizes the mutual benefit accruing
from employees taking vacations and, as a general policy, will insist that they do so. At the same time the
Company, because of customer requirements, reserves the right at its sole discretion to pay the employee in
lieu of vacation if conditions so warrant.

**Section 3.** Employees with more than six (6) months service who are laid off due to lack of
work, shall receive vacation pay pro-rated on the number of months actually worked on the basis of
one-twelfth (1/12) of the vacation pay for each month actually worked. New hires shall receive a
pro-rated portion of vacation based on one-twelfth (1/12) of the vacation for each month remaining
with the calendar year hired, including the month for which hired.

**Section 4.** An employee who voluntarily quits, or who dies during the contract year, without
having received his or her vacation, shall receive vacation pay pro-rated on the number of months
actually worked on the basis of one-twelfth (1/12) of the vacation pay for each month actually
worked. Provided however, that, in the case of the employee who quits, he or she shall not be
entitled to such vacation pay unless he or she has given the company at least seven (7) days notice in
writing, of intent to quit, and further provided that this section shall not apply to those employees
with less than one year of continuous service with the Company.

**Section 5.** An employee who retires shall be entitled to full vacation pay at time of retirement.

## ARTICLE 15 Reporting Time

Employees required to report at a regular starting time or any time outside of regularly scheduled
hours and place and who through no fault of their own are not permitted to work on their assigned job shall
be given, whenever possible, a full day’s work without affecting any regular employee. If work is not
available and the employee has not been properly advised of a layoff or a change in work schedule, making
reporting necessary, he or she will be given four (4) hours work or four (4) hours pay at his/her regular rate
of pay. However, the Company will not be liable for occurrences which are beyond its control, including
acts of God, or power shutdown or shortage of gas or electricity or strike in which the Company’s employees
are not involved. The Company agrees to make an honest sincere effort to notify employees sufficiently in
advance to avoid useless reporting for work on such occasions.

In the event the Company’s plant or equipment is damaged by fire, tornado, riot or civil commotion, or if a strike in which the company's employees are not involved causes a suspension of production, the Company, through mutual agreement with the Unions, during such emergency may recall to work, such employees as would ordinarily do the work required to ready the plant and equipment in order to resume production.

Production employees shall be given a minimum of two hours notice when they are required to work overtime except that employees shall be given notice for Saturday overtime by 2:00 p.m. on Thursday. If the Company cancels Saturday overtime, it will give notice by 3:00 p.m. on Friday. Maintenance Departments and tool room department employees may be required to work overtime without notice.

## ARTICLE 16 Bulletin Boards

A bulletin board shall be provided in a conspicuous place within the confines of the plant property, where notices of the Union meetings and other official Union business may be posted from time to time.

## ARTICLE 17 Sanitary Toilet and Washroom Facilities

The Company will furnish and maintain satisfactory toilet facilities, wash bowls, lockers and satisfactory cool drinking water in sanitary drinking fountains in convenient places wherever needed.

## ARTICLE 18 Pay Days

Employees will be paid on a bi-weekly basis every other Friday.

## ARTICLE 19 Wash-Up Period

All employees covered by this agreement shall be allowed five (5) minutes time before noon and five (5) minutes before quitting time each day.

## ARTICLE 20 Change of Working Conditions, Hours or Pay by Law

It is further understood and agreed between the Company and the Union that if at any time during the period of this agreement any condition of employment as set forth herein should be changed by law either below or above the hours stipulated herein, the affected provision of this agreement shall automatically be changed so as to fully comply with said law, and the parties herein shall immediately enter into negotiations for the purpose of readjusting the question of hours and rates of pay to be paid thereafter.

## ARTICLE 21 Supervisory and Managerial Limitation

Supervisory employees shall not be permitted to perform work on any bargaining unit job except in the following types of situations:

1) In the instruction or training of employees.

2)   In the performance of necessary work to determine the troubles when production
     difficulties are encountered on a job.

3)   In assisting with experimental or try-out work on which the engineering has been
     completed or which is not in production as yet.

4)   In the performance of maintaining safety requirements.

5)   Will at no time exceed ten percent of supervisor’s total time.

## ARTICLE 22 Employee Consideration

   Employees who have rendered faithful and honest service and who have become unable to handle
heavy work will be given consideration for such light work as may be available from time to time, where
they shall be physically and mentally capable of performing such work, in which case such employee shall be
paid the rate then being paid in the job classification to which he or she is assigned.

## ARTICLE 23 Temporary Transfer at Convenience of Company

(a)  The Company may transfer employees to fill temporary positions caused by
sickness, absences, vacations, unusual amount of work, etc. Experience gained from such
temporary transfer will be useful when bidding on permanent job postings.

All temporary transfers that are made for the convenience of the Company will, after such
Temporary transfer, be returned to their original place of work.

(b)  **Temporary Transfers Without Posting.** The company is not required to post temporary
positions nor fill such positions by seniority, if such assignment is one month or less in duration. Preference
will be given to employees in the bargaining unit where the temporary position sits.

(c)  **Temporary Transfers with Posting.** If the temporary transfer is to replace a regular employee
who is absent because of illness, accident or leave of absence, and such replacement extends for one (1)
month or more, the Company will, at the end of one (1) month post the job on the bulletin board for twenty-
four (24) working hours, designating it as a temporary transfer. Such employees who bid on such job
(temporary transfer) shall be selected according to seniority, provided that they are qualified, and shall be
given twenty (20) working days trial period. Preference will be given to employees in the bargaining unit
where the temporary position sits. It is understood that when the regular employee returns, then whoever has
bid on and obtained such job shall go back to his or her permanent classification. Holidays and vacations
occurring during this temporary transfer with posting will be paid at the higher rate.

Provisions concerning holiday and vacation pay apply only to those employees who work
full-time at a single rate and do not apply to those who work part-time at various rates.

(d) Pay. It is understood and agreed by both parties to this agreement that in the event an employee is temporarily transferred for the convenience of the Company from his or her permanent position to another position which pays a higher rate of pay than his or her permanent position, the employee shall receive the rate of pay as is paid on the position to which temporarily transferred; and in an event an employee is temporarily transferred from his or her permanent position to another position which pays a lower rate of pay than his or her permanent position, he or she shall receive the rate of pay for the time worked on such temporary position as is regularly paid on the employee’s permanent position.

An employee temporarily working at a high-rated job for five (5) working days immediately prior to a holiday shall receive holiday pay at such rate, however, that to be eligible to receive holiday pay he or she must work the regularly scheduled workday before and after the holiday, as provided in paragraph (2), Section 5 of Article 13.

An employee, who vacations while temporarily working a high-rated job full-time for a period of at least two (2) consecutive months immediately prior to his or her vacation, shall receive vacation pay at the higher rate. Employees not qualifying for the above will receive their permanent rate.

(e) Flex Assignments. Nothing in this Agreement prohibits the Company from requiring a machinist to perform assembly and/or packing on the product he or she manufactures. Nor does anything in the Agreement prohibit the Company from requiring an an employee from performing a lower-paid job for a temporary period, provided the employee continues to be paid at his or her existing rate.

## ARTICLE 24 Night Shift

1. All jobs will be posted as permanent.

2. The employee selected through job posting for night-shift work will remain on that shift until he or she is the successful bidder on a job posted for day-shift work, or until the night shift is terminated. However, night-shift employees may file written application for dayshift work in the lowest job classification and at the rate of that classification. Such applications will be kept on file for a period of six (6) months, and those employees who filed for transfer to day shift will be given preference for openings on the dayshift not requiring a job posting. In the event more than one employee files written application for transfer, seniority on the night shift shall prevail. The Company may refuse to honor such written request for transfer if the employee has had a shift transfer within the past six months. Nothing in this paragraph will prohibit an employee from applying at any time for dayshift work in the lowest classification should unusual circumstances prohibit his or her continuance of night-shift work. If night-shift employees are needed for inventory purposes, they will receive their nightshift rate less shift premium pay.

3. In the event the night shift is terminated, employees on night shift will be eligible for dayshift work in their classification. If as the result of the integration of the shifts, a reduction in the number of employees in a job classification is necessary, job classification seniority shall prevail, and the employee assigned to work in another classification will be paid the rate of the classification to which he or she is assigned.

4. In the event the Company finds it necessary to assign a day-shift employee to night-shift work (for example to break in inexperienced help, or when a job posting results in no applicants), such transfer will be temporary. At the time of assignment, the employee will be notified of the duration of the temporary transfer.

## ARTICLE 25 Reduction in Working Force

Where the Company is faced with either a reduction in the working force or a reduction in the number of days to be worked in the work week, the Company may go to a four (4) day week so long as it does not exceed eight (8) weeks in any one contract year. Thereafter, within the contract year, the Company shall layoff, if necessary, all employees with less tenure than three (3) years in the order of their seniority. If still further cuts in employment are necessary, the decrease in the working force shall be made in accordance with Article 8 of this contract. Where, as the result of layoffs, employees are assigned to other job classifications, they shall receive the rate of pay of the job classification to which assigned. The exception to this is an employee within the Machinist Union with ten (10) or more years of seniority. Those employees meeting this qualification will not be reduced in pay below the rate of the Group III Operator, regardless of the lower classification to which assigned. This provision shall not apply where the Company shuts down on a Monday when the Tuesday following is celebrated as a holiday, nor a Friday when the previous day (Thursday) is celebrated as a holiday, or a sufficient number of days to take inventory.

It is understood that in the case where an employee, because of reduction in classification, is moved back to the lowest paying classification, that same employee will become and remain the top bidder on the Temporary Transfer List within the job classification vacated without respect to seniority. It is also understood that if former position needs to be filled as permanent with one (1) year of employee's reduction, same employee will automatically fill position if permanent posting is signed, without regard to seniority. The original date of acquiring former position before reduction would be reinstated as classification seniority date. This paragraph does not pertain to employee who lost position because of being laid off.

## Discrepancies and Verification Notes

The following items should be checked against the signed/original contract or clarified with the Company/Union before the text is treated as authoritative:

1. **Weekend overtime cancellation deadline.** Article 13, Section 3(c) states that weekend overtime must be cancelled by **noon the day prior**. Article 15 states that cancelled Saturday overtime will be announced by **3:00 p.m. Friday**. These deadlines conflict.

2. **Mandatory weekend overtime notice.** Article 13, Section 3(b) requires notice of mandatory weekend overtime two days prior and gives examples for first and second shifts. Article 13, Section 7 separately states that mandatory weekend overtime will be posted by **2:00 p.m. Thursday**. These provisions may not cover exactly the same circumstances, but the wording should be checked for consistency.

3. **Vacation call-in language versus the separate Attendance Policy.** Article 14, Section 1 states that employees may use five vacation days or ten half-days, or a combination, with notice no later than before shift or mid-shift. The separate Attendance Policy states that the CBA permits up to five full-day vacation days with at least one hour's notice and three emergency call-ins of less than one hour per year. The latter wording does not appear in the supplied CBA transcription and should be verified against the signed agreement, any side letter, or amendment.

4. **Absence/job-abandonment thresholds.** Article 8(j) allows potential discipline when an employee is absent for two consecutive days without Company consent, subject to sickness and other justifiable-cause provisions. The separate Attendance Policy defines job abandonment as failure to call in for three consecutive days of unexcused absence. These can potentially coexist, but the distinction between discipline and automatic job abandonment should be confirmed.

5. **Older Policies and Regulations call-in rule.** Policy/Regulation 37 says an employee must provide a bona fide reason on the first day of absence and every third day thereafter and phone before the start of the shift. The separate Attendance Policy requires notice at least one hour before the scheduled start time for unscheduled absences or tardies. The relationship between these rules should be verified.

6. **Third-shift premium notation.** Article 13, Section 6 states that third-shift employees receive **“.40¢ per hour”**. Literally, that notation is four-tenths of one cent per hour; it may have been intended to mean **$0.40 (40¢) per hour**. The source should be checked. The transcription preserves the printed amount rather than guessing.

7. **Article 14 vacation wording.** The source contains overlapping language governing daily vacation, half-day vacation, and five days/ten half-days with short notice. The exact relationship between the five-day advance-approval provisions and the later short-notice provision should be confirmed if the app will calculate eligibility automatically.

8. **Policies and Regulations — “causes for discharge.”** The appendix introduces all 48 listed rules as “causes for discharge,” although some individual rules themselves describe warnings or other outcomes. The wording is preserved, but the disciplinary interaction with Article 6's just-cause requirement should not be inferred by the app without human interpretation.
