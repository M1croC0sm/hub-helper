# Work Schedule Presets

These schedules were supplied by the user on August 16, 2026. They are initial
defaults for attendance entry and time comparisons, not policy documents.

## First Shift

- Regular: Monday through Friday, 6:00 AM–2:30 PM
- Weekday overtime when active: 5:00 AM–2:30 PM
- Saturday overtime when active: 5:00 AM–11:00 AM

## Second Shift

- Regular: Monday through Thursday, 2:15 PM–12:45 AM the following day
- Friday overtime when active: 2:15 PM–8:15 PM
- Other weekday overtime: unknown

## Application Behavior

- The user selects a preset during setup and can edit it later.
- Overtime schedules are applied only when the user marks overtime active for a
  date; the app must not infer that overtime is active every week.
- A schedule override can be attached to a specific date without changing the
  preset.
- Second-shift end times after midnight belong to the shift that began on the
  preceding scheduled day.
